Always StarTagger.

One word. Capital S and T. The name stays together on one line. “Stargaze” and “Star Tagger” are earlier public names to retire when the identity is applied.

A monochrome sky

Inter, with intention

The same family carries the app and its feature: Inter 650 for StarTagger, Inter 600 for Astral Harness. Large, closely spaced headlines create presence; reading text stays open and comfortable.

Build the identity

The app and feature identities now have reusable SVG files, placement studies, and working motion previews. The next step is to apply the reviewed system to the product and launch materials.

  1. Review the app and feature system.

    Review the LED lockups, Astral Harness focus frame, and the loading entrance in their intended placements.

  2. Apply the name consistently.

    Update public app names, metadata, accessible labels, and credits to StarTagger. Capture the verified product with the new identity.

  3. Create the first marketing family.

    Develop social, share-image, and presentation compositions. Confirm channels and export requirements before final production.
